How It Works

1
Sign up.

Every Studio Gang staffer can sign up with their name and the office they're playing for.

2
Two leagues from four offices.

Once sign-ups close, the four offices are paired into two leagues — each league is two offices joined as one.

3
Eight per league.

Each league fields eight players at the tournament. The winning league earns the bragging rights — and honour — of its two offices.

4
Proportional quotas.

Each office's share of its league's eight seats scales with how many of its people signed up.

Example: Chicago signs up 10 and San Francisco signs up 5. Their joint league of 8 fills with 5 from Chicago and 3 from San Francisco.

5
The gamemaster picks the roster.

Everyone travels to Wandawega — but within each office, a coordinator known as the gamemaster chooses which of the sign-ups will actually take the table.
